# POP – Preparation of Extract Aliquots for Differential Liquid–Liquid Partitioning

## Objective

Standardize the preparation of extract aliquots for the Differential Liquid–Liquid Partitioning workflow used in DAFdiscovery experiments.

This procedure generates identical aliquots of crude extract that will subsequently be submitted to multiple solvent partition systems for the calculation of apparent partition coefficients and differential concentration profiles.

---

# Materials

* Crude dry extract
* Analytical balance (0.1 mg resolution)
* 16 mL glass tubes with screw caps
* Methanol (LC-MS grade)
* Ultrapure water
* Micropipettes and tips
* Vortex mixer
* Sample identification labels

---

# Sample Identification

For each extract, prepare one partition tube for each solvent system.

# Procedure

## 1. Extract Homogenization

Before weighing:

* Homogenize the dry extract thoroughly.
* Break up any aggregates.
* Minimize exposure to air and humidity.

---

## 2. Preparation of Stock Solution

1. Weigh **110.0 ± 0.5 mg** of dry extract into a 16 mL tube.
2. Add **5.5 mL** of appropriate solvent.

Typical solvent:

* Methanol : Water (1:1, v/v)

3. Vortex until complete dissolution.

Final concentration:

**20 mg/mL**

---

## 3. Aliquot Preparation

Transfer:

**1,000 μL**

of stock solution into each partition tube.

Each aliquot contains approximately:

**20 mg of extract**

---

## 4. Solvent Contribution Correction

The stock solution contributes solvent to the partition system.

For a stock solution prepared in MeOH:H₂O (1:1):

Each 1,000 μL aliquot contains:

* 500 μL Methanol
* 500 μL Water

These volumes must be subtracted from the final solvent composition of the partition system.

# Expected Output

For each crude extract:

* 5 partition systems
* 20 mg extract per system
* Identical starting concentration
* Suitable for liquid-liquid partitioning experiments

---

# Quality Control

Before proceeding to partitioning:

* Confirm complete extract dissolution.
* Verify tube identification.
* Confirm aliquot volume.
* Inspect for precipitates.
* Record all masses and volumes in the experiment log.

---

# Notes

This protocol was designed for the DAFdiscovery Differential Liquid–Liquid Partitioning workflow, where multiple solvent systems are used to generate concentration gradients and estimate apparent partition coefficients (Kapp) for chemical and biological profiling.
